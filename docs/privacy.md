# Privacy Policy

**Affiliate Agent**

**Last updated: September 1, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how information may be processed when Affiliate Agent is used.

Affiliate Agent is a software project for creating and publishing user-approved product content to connected third-party services.

## 1. Information processed

Depending on the workflow and enabled integrations, the application may process:

* product URLs supplied by the user
* product-related information retrieved during research
* text content generated for social-media publication
* images and videos selected or generated for publication
* identifiers required to communicate with connected third-party platforms
* authentication tokens required for authorized API access
* technical information required to execute publication jobs

## 2. Credentials and authentication

Authentication credentials and access tokens required for privileged operations are intended to be stored by the local execution component.

They are not intended to be included in public job payloads, AI prompts or generated social-media content.

The AI agent does not receive private credentials such as social-network access credentials, Amazon partner credentials or storage access keys.

## 3. Third-party services

Affiliate Agent may communicate with third-party services when the user has enabled and authorized the corresponding integration.

Potential services include, depending on the configuration:

* Amazon
* TikTok
* Instagram
* other social-network platforms
* cloud storage or infrastructure services required by the application

Each third-party service processes information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

## 4. Social-network publication

When the user explicitly approves content for publication, the application may transmit the approved content, media and information required by the corresponding API to the selected third-party service.

The application is designed to publish only to accounts and services that have been explicitly configured and authorized for use.

## 5. Storage

The application may temporarily or persistently store:

* job information
* media metadata
* media files
* publication results
* technical execution information

Retention depends on the configured application workflow and storage policies.

## 6. User responsibility

Users are responsible for ensuring that content and media submitted for publication may legally be used for the intended purpose and that they have the necessary rights and permissions.

## 7. Data security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect credentials and other sensitive information.

In particular, privileged credentials are kept separate from public job data and AI-generated content.

No security system can guarantee absolute protection against unauthorized access.

## 8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its integrations change.

The latest version will be published on this website.
